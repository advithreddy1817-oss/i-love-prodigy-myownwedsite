why i love prodigy

Prodigy is a free-to-play, fantasy-style online role-playing game (RPG) designed to help children practice school subjects like math and English while they play.

How It Works

    • Wizard Characters: Kids create and customize their own wizard avatar to explore a magical 3D world.
      Battles and Quests: Players go on quests, collect pets, and battle monsters or other players, much like Pokémon.
      Answering Questions: To cast spells and win battles, children must correctly answer educational questions.
      Adaptive Learning: The game adjusts question difficulty in real time based on each child's performance and grade level (grades 1 through 8 for math).
